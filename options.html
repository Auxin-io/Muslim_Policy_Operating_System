Muslim Policy OS — Settings

This extension uses Google Gemini's free API tier. The key is stored only in this browser's local extension storage and is sent only to generativelanguage.googleapis.com.

Get a free key at aistudio.google.com/apikey. The free tier has daily rate limits but no cost.

The toolbar icon always shows a badge (!) when a bill is found — this adds an optional pop-up notification on top of that, only for higher-severity direct matches, to avoid alert fatigue from routine scans.

How auto-scan works: every time you visit a page, the extension reads its visible text locally in your browser and checks it against a list of legislative-sounding keywords (bill, act, senate, ordinance, statute, etc.) — this check never leaves your computer. Only pages that pass this check are sent to Gemini for full analysis, which keeps routine browsing (email, shopping, social media) from using your API quota. You can turn auto-scan off entirely from the toggle in the popup, or force-analyze any individual page with "Analyze Anyway" even if the local check found nothing.

Privacy exclusions: webmail (Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo Mail, Proton, iCloud), banking/payment sites, healthcare/patient portals, and private-messaging sites (Messenger, WhatsApp Web, Telegram Web) are never auto-scanned at all — the extension skips reading their content entirely, regardless of what the keyword filter would have matched. This runs before any page text is read, so it's not something the keyword filter alone could catch.

What this tool does: it identifies any law or bill discussed on a page and summarizes Muslim Impact (how it could affect Muslim communities), Muslim Exposure (who specifically may be affected), Possible Actions (general civic steps), and Key Deadlines (only when explicitly stated in the source). All output is AI-generated analysis for research and advocacy purposes — it is not legal advice, and any contact details, vote counts, or dates shown are only reproduced when they appear explicitly in your source text, never invented.
